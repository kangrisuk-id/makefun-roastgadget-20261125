## Judge brief checklist (criteria in order — first difference decides)

1. **Daily gadget/app targeted** — names or clearly targets a gadget/app people use every day; a line that never does loses to any line that does.
2. **Actually funny** — lands on a first read, sting of recognition, not a mild grumble.
3. **Fresh** — angle, comparison or detail not seen before; not the obvious complaint everyone makes.
4. **Neatly worded** — tight, well timed, no wasted words, comfortably inside 200 chars.
5. **One person's specific gripe** — concrete detail about how the thing actually behaves, not a generic complaint.

Crudeness for its own sake does not count. Scoring: each criterion 1–5, total /25. Character counts verified by script (all 40 lines ≤ 200 chars; longest is 112).

## Devices used (10)

- **P** Personification — gadget as needy ex / boss / teacher
- **V** Versus frame — gadget vs. something
- **N** Concrete number — a specific count that makes it real
- **R** Performance review — the gadget's annual appraisal
- **D** Dialogue — quoted speech, the gadget talking
- **L** Absurd literalism — taking the gadget's behaviour literally
- **B** Backhanded compliment — praise that stings
- **T** Rule of three — three-beat rhythm
- **A** Second-person accusation — "You had one job…"
- **C** Cause→effect chain — one small act, escalating consequences
